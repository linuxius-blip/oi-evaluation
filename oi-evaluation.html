How open is your innovation?

Most firms talk about open innovation. Far fewer have a clear picture of which modes they actually use, which they neglect, and what their pattern is costing them.

This evaluation maps your organization across four modes — sourcing, acquiring, selling, and revealing — and reads the pattern back to you.

These questions are about firm-level patterns over the past few years — how your organization is configured for open innovation as a whole, not how any single project or initiative played out.
